Topik 1: Interaction Design

UCD & NORMAN PRINCIPLES

Memahami User-Centered Design (UCD), perbedaan Usability Goals (efektivitas, efisiensi, safety, learnability) vs User Experience Goals (menyenangkan, estetik, rewarding), serta 6 prinsip desain fundamental Donald Norman.

Topik 2: Conceptualizing Interaction

MENTAL MODELS & PARADIGMS

Model konseptual versus mental model pengguna, antarmuka berbasis metafora (desktop, shopping cart), serta 4 tipe interaksi utama: Instructing, Conversing, Manipulating, dan Exploring.

Topik 3: Cognitive Aspects

FITTS' LAW & HICK'S LAW

Keterbatasan atensi manusia, persepsi visual, memori kerja vs jangka panjang, Cognitive Load Theory, formulasi Fitts' Law (waktu pointing) dan Hick's Law (waktu pengambilan keputusan).

Pelajari Topik 3 →
👥

Topik 4: Social Interaction

CSCW & TELEPRESENCE

Mekanisme kolaborasi manusia-komputer: Computer-Supported Cooperative Work (CSCW), matriks waktu-tempat (Same/Different Time/Place), Social Presence, Telepresence, dan norma komunitas online.

Pelajari Topik 4 →

Topik 5: Emotional Interaction

VISCERAL, BEHAVIORAL, REFLECTIVE

Teori 3 tingkatan emosional Donald Norman: Visceral (estetika sensoris awal), Behavioral (kenyamanan fungsionalitas), dan Reflective (identitas diri dan kebanggaan), serta peran Affective Computing.

Topik 6: Interface Types

CLI TO BCI PARADIGMS

Evolusi antarmuka: Command Line Interface (CLI), GUI, Natural User Interface (NUI), Voice User Interface (VUI), Virtual/Augmented Reality (AR/VR), Wearables, Tangible UI, dan Brain-Computer Interface (BCI).
